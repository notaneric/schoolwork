Solubility: like dissolves like

  • Polar and ionic substances dissolve in polar water. Nonpolar substances dissolve in nonpolar solvents like hexane.
  • Hexane (C6H14) and water do not mix. They separate into two layers.
  • I2 is nonpolar, so it dissolves in the hexane layer. KMnO4 is ionic, so it dissolves in the water layer, held there by ion-dipole forces between the ions and the polar water molecules.
